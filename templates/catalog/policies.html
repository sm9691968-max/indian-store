About Indian Store

Indian Store is a curated marketplace for premium keyboards, audio, displays and desk gear. Every listing is sourced through authorised distributors and checked for authenticity before it ships.

Shipping & delivery

Orders are dispatched from our Bengaluru and Mumbai fulfilment centres within one working day. Metro pincodes are typically delivered the next day; most other addresses arrive within two to five working days.

  • Delivery is free on orders over {{ free_delivery_label }}.
  • Below that, a flat delivery fee of {{ shipping_flat|money }} is added at checkout and shown before you pay.
  • Cash on delivery is available on orders up to {{ (2000)|money }} in most serviceable pincodes.
  • Large items such as displays are delivered by a two-person team with a doorstep inspection.

Returns & refunds

You have seven days from delivery to request a return on an unused item in its original packaging. Start the return from the order page and we will arrange a pickup where serviceable.

  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within three to five working days of our receiving the item.
  • Gift-card balances used on the order are restored immediately.
  • Items damaged in transit are replaced at no cost — share photos when you raise the request.

Warranty

Every product carries the manufacturer's warranty listed on its product page under the specification sheet. Your GST invoice is your proof of purchase, so keep it safe.

  • Warranty service is handled through authorised brand service centres across India.
  • Extended protection plans, where offered, are shown at checkout on eligible products.

Payments & EMI

We accept UPI, Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, net banking and cash on delivery. Card details never touch our servers — every transaction is processed over a PCI-DSS compliant gateway with 256-bit encryption.

  • No-cost EMI is available on eligible cards and shown on the product page for orders above the EMI threshold.
  • Prices include GST; a GST invoice is generated automatically for every order.

Privacy policy

We collect only what is needed to fulfil your order — your name, contact details, delivery address and order history. We never sell your personal data.

  • Payment details are handled by our payment partners and are never stored on our servers.
  • Analytics cookies stay off until you accept them from the cookie notice.
  • You can request a copy or the deletion of your account data at any time by opening a support ticket.

Terms of sale

By placing an order you confirm that the details you provide are accurate and that you are authorised to use the selected payment method.

  • Prices and availability are subject to change until your order is confirmed.
  • Orders may be cancelled until they are marked Shipped; after that, please start a return.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of India, with jurisdiction in the courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka.
